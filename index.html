How Does the US Salary Calculator Work?

This calculator estimates your take-home pay using your annual salary, US state and federal filing status.

1. Federal Income Tax

Federal income tax is calculated using progressive tax brackets. Your taxable income can fall into multiple brackets rather than being taxed at one rate.

2. State Income Tax

State income tax varies significantly across the United States. Some states have no individual income tax, while others use progressive or flat tax systems.

3. Social Security and Medicare

The estimate also includes common employee payroll taxes, including Social Security and Medicare.

4. Take-Home Pay

Estimated take-home pay is your gross salary minus the estimated taxes included in this calculator.

Why Does Your State Matter?

Two employees earning the same salary can have different take-home pay because state income tax rules vary.

Your actual paycheck can also depend on deductions, benefits, retirement contributions, credits, local taxes and other circumstances that are not included in this simplified calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this salary calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is designed to be free to use.

Does this calculator work for all 50 states?

Yes. You can select any of the 50 US states. States without a general individual income tax are represented with no state income tax in the estimate.

Is the result exact?

No. This is an estimate for planning and educational purposes. Your actual paycheck and tax liability can differ.

Does the calculator include Social Security?

Yes. The calculator includes an estimated employee Social Security contribution based on the 2026 wage base.

Does it include Medicare?

Yes. The estimate includes regular Medicare tax and, where applicable, an Additional Medicare Tax estimate.
