About US Salary Calculator

US Salary Calculator is a free online tool designed to help people estimate their potential take-home pay based on salary, state and federal filing status.

The calculator is intended for general educational and planning purposes. It helps users understand how federal taxes, state taxes and common payroll taxes can affect gross income.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make salary and take-home-pay information easier to understand through a simple, fast and accessible calculator.

Important

Results are estimates and should not be treated as tax, accounting, legal or financial advice. Actual tax liability and paycheck amounts may vary based on individual circumstances.
