About Us

Welcome to US Salary Calculator, a free online tool designed to help people estimate their take-home pay based on salary, state, and federal filing status.

Our goal is to make salary calculations easier to understand by presenting estimated federal taxes, state taxes, Social Security, Medicare, and estimated take-home pay in a simple format.

What We Provide

Our website provides salary calculator tools for all 50 U.S. states. Users can enter an annual gross salary, select their state and filing status, and receive an estimated breakdown of their income and taxes.

We also provide educational information to help users better understand how salary and payroll taxes can affect their estimated take-home pay.

Our Approach

We aim to keep our calculators simple, accessible, and easy to use. We regularly review the information used by the calculators and improve the website when updates are needed.

Salary calculations can vary depending on individual circumstances, including deductions, benefits, credits, local taxes, and other payroll factors. For this reason, the results shown on this website should be treated as estimates rather than official tax calculations.

Important Information

US Salary Calculator is an informational website. The calculators are not intended to provide tax, financial, legal, or accounting advice.

For decisions involving your personal tax situation, consider consulting a qualified tax professional or reviewing information from the appropriate government authorities.
